Privacy policy

Rizvi Builds Publisher · 22 November 2026

Rizvi Builds Publisher is a small tool that Shahzaib Rizvi runs on his own computer to upload his own videos to his own YouTube channel, @rizvibuilds. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the YouTube permissions you grant at sign-in, it uploads videos, sets their titles, descriptions and publish times, and reads the channel's name and the privacy status of its uploads to confirm a video went up correctly.

What it stores

The sign-in token is stored only on that one computer, in a private file. No data is sent to any server other than Google's own, nothing is sold or shared, and no information about viewers or other users is collected.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
